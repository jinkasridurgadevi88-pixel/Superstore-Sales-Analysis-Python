7\. Meaningful Business Insights



\### 1. Regional Performance



\- The West region generated the highest profit, approximately $108,418.

\- The East region generated approximately $91,523 in profit.

\- The Central region had the lowest regional profit, approximately $39,706.

\- The West and East regions together contributed around 69.81% of total profit.



\*\*Business understanding:\*\* The West and East regions are important contributors to overall profitability. The Central region could be examined further to understand differences in sales, discounts, product mix and costs.



\### 2. Category Performance



\- Technology generated the highest sales, contributing 36.4% of total sales.

\- Technology contributed approximately 50.79% of total profit.

\- Office Supplies contributed approximately 42.77% of total profit.

\- Furniture contributed approximately 6.44% of total profit.



\*\*Business understanding:\*\* Technology was the strongest category in terms of profit contribution. Furniture had a much smaller share of total profit, so its pricing, discounts and costs may need further investigation.



\### 3. Customer Segment Analysis



\- Consumer customers contributed approximately 50.56% of total sales.

\- Corporate customers contributed approximately 30.74%.

\- Home Office customers contributed approximately 18.70%.



\*\*Business understanding:\*\* Consumer customers were the largest sales-contributing segment. The business could focus on maintaining customer relationships while also exploring opportunities to grow Corporate and Home Office sales.



\### 4. State-wise Profitability



\- California generated the highest state-wise profit, approximately $76,381.

\- New York was another major contributor, generating approximately $74,039.

\- Texas had the lowest state-wise profit.



\*\*Business understanding:\*\* State-level analysis helps identify locations with strong and weak profitability. Further investigation into pricing, discount levels and product demand could help explain these differences.



\### 5. Shipping Mode Analysis



\- Standard Class contributed approximately 57.29% of total profit.

\- Second Class contributed approximately 20.06%.

\- First Class contributed approximately 17.10%.

\- Same Day contributed approximately 5.55%.



\*\*Business understanding:\*\* Standard Class contributed the largest share of profit. However, profit contribution alone does not indicate shipping efficiency. Shipping costs, delivery time and customer satisfaction would also need to be considered.



\### 6. Product Performance



\- Canon imageCLASS 2200 Advanced Copier was the highest sales-contributing product.

\- It contributed approximately 2.68% of total sales.

\- The same product also contributed approximately 8.80% of total profit.



\*\*Business understanding:\*\* This product was a significant contributor to both sales and profit. Monitoring its demand and stock availability could be useful for business planning.
